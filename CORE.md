# FIVE — Core

## Core idea

FIVE is an AI-assisted singing coach built around one simple rule:

**Become a singer in five songs.**

You always have **YOUR FIVE SONGS**.

They are yours.

They are the songs you choose, believe in, work on, change, come back to and eventually own.

They are not a scoreboard.

They are the five songs through which the coach understands your voice and helps you become a better singer.

When you want to know whether you have improved, you sing one of **YOUR FIVE SONGS** again and compare that performance with your own earlier version of that same song.

## How it starts

You choose five songs you believe suit your voice.

You sing a short section of each.

That gives the AI coach its first understanding of:

- where you feel strong
- what kind of voice you think you have
- what you naturally choose
- where you struggle
- where you sound most like yourself

Your first five songs are your belief.

## Your Five Songs are the course

Everything in the app connects back to **YOUR FIVE SONGS**.

Exercises, feedback, listening sessions, technique, experiments, tryouts and challenges only exist to help you:

**understand, improve, choose or own your five songs.**

The songs are not examples inside the course.

**YOUR FIVE SONGS are the course.**

## Your Five Songs can always change

You choose them together with the AI coach.

You can replace one whenever another song becomes more useful, more revealing, more challenging or simply more yours.

The coach can suggest a change.

You can challenge a slot yourself.

Your five songs should evolve as your voice and your understanding of it evolve.

But you always have five.

## Progress

Progress is song-specific.

If you want to test whether you improved, sing any one of **YOUR FIVE SONGS** again.

The coach compares that performance to your earlier attempts at that same song and tells you what changed.

The question is always:

**Can you sing this song better than before?**

Over time, your five songs become living proof of your development.

## Graduation

You do not complete a generic level.

You prove that you can sing **YOUR FIVE SONGS** with control, confidence, technique, interpretation and your own voice.

The five you graduate with do not have to be the five you started with.

They are the five songs that best prove who you have become as a singer.

## The Sixth

The Sixth is the graduation test.

It checks whether what you learned through your five songs transfers to something new.

It tests both you and the coach:

**Have you become a better singer?  
Does the coach actually understand your voice?**

## Visual principle

The mouth is the constant. The hand is the expression.

**YOUR FIVE SONGS can each have their own hand gesture.**

The five fingers stay present, but the gesture changes with how the song feels and how you sing it: open, tight, floating, pushing, hiding, gripping an invisible microphone, reaching, releasing.

The gesture is not decoration. It reflects the vocal and emotional character of that song.

**Five songs. Five gestures. One voice.**

## Core loop

**Choose → Sing → Discover → Train → Retry → Compare → Change your Five → Own your Five → Graduate**

## Product rule

If something does not help you:

**understand, improve, choose or own YOUR FIVE SONGS, it does not belong in FIVE.**
