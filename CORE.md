# FIVE — Core

## Core idea

FIVE is an AI-assisted singing coach built around one simple rule:

**Become a singer in five songs.**

You always have five active songs.

Those five songs are your current repertoire — the material through which the coach understands you, teaches you, and helps you improve.

They are not a scoreboard.

You do not constantly chase one overall number.

When you want to know whether you have improved, you sing one of your Five again and compare that performance with your own earlier version of that same song.

## How it starts

You choose five songs you believe suit your voice.

You sing a short section of each.

That gives the AI coach its first understanding of:

- where you feel strong
- what kind of voice you think you have
- what you naturally choose
- where you struggle
- where you sound most like yourself

The first Five are your belief.

## The Five are the course

Everything in the app connects back to your current five songs.

Exercises, feedback, listening sessions, technique, experiments, tryouts and challenges only exist to help you:

**understand, improve, choose or master your Five.**

The songs are not examples inside the course.

**The songs are the course.**

## The Five can always change

You choose the Five together with the AI coach.

You can replace a song whenever another one becomes more useful, more revealing, more challenging or simply more yours.

The coach can suggest a change.

You can challenge a slot yourself.

The Five should evolve as your voice and your understanding of it evolve.

But there are always five.

## Progress

Progress is song-specific.

If you want to test whether you improved, sing any one of your Five again.

The coach compares that performance to your earlier attempts at that song and tells you what changed.

The question is always:

**Can you sing this song better than before?**

Over time, the Five become living proof of your development.

## Graduation

You do not complete a generic level.

You prove that you can sing five songs with control, confidence, technique, interpretation and your own voice.

The final Five are not necessarily the same five you started with.

They are the five songs that best prove who you have become as a singer.

## The Sixth

The Sixth is the graduation test.

It checks whether what you learned through the Five transfers to something new.

It tests both you and the coach:

**Have you become a better singer?  
Does the coach actually understand your voice?**

## Core loop

**Choose → Sing → Discover → Train → Retry → Compare → Change the Five → Own the Five → Graduate**

## Product rule

If something does not help you:

**understand, improve, choose or own your Five, it does not belong in FIVE.**
